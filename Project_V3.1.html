The library, running quietly in the background.

Granthalaya keeps the catalogue, the shelves, and the people who borrow from them in sync — built to fit a school library on Monday and a university stack of forty thousand titles by Friday.

Everything the front desk and the back room need

Cataloguing and lending are only half the job. Granthalaya treats the physical stock — copies, condition, shelf location — as seriously as the records about who's reading what.

Catalogue & search

Add, edit, and retire titles in seconds. Search by title, author, category, or accession number, and see live availability before a student walks to the shelf.

Inventory control

Every copy is tracked, not just every title — shelf location, condition, and restock alerts when a section runs low.

Issue & return desk

Scan or search a member and a book, and the due date, availability count, and history update together — no double entry.

Two front doors

Students and staff sign in to purpose-built views — one for browsing and borrowing, one for running the collection.

Overdue & availability tracking

Colour-coded due dates surface overdue copies automatically, and the moment a book comes back, it reappears in the catalogue as available.

How a single copy moves through the system

The same lifecycle applies whether it's a paperback in a school library or a reference volume in a university archive.

01

Catalogued

A title and its copies are added, with category and accession details.

02

Shelved

Inventory records the shelf location and condition of each physical copy.

03

Issued

A member borrows a copy; availability and due date update instantly.

04

Returned

The copy is checked back in and its condition reconfirmed.

05

Reconciled

Transaction history and stock counts stay accurate for the next report.

Built to be reconfigured, not rebuilt

The same catalogue, inventory, and lending engine — set up differently depending on who's running the room.

School

Class-set lending

Issue textbooks by class and section, track class-set copies separately from the general shelf, and keep an eye on end-of-term returns.

College & university

High-volume stacks

Thousands of titles across departments, longer loan periods for research material, and staff accounts scoped to a section of the collection.

Public & community

Open membership

Walk-in registration, mixed-format collections, and a catalogue built for browsing rather than searching for a syllabus item.
